VIDEO FRAME: the TARGET is a single frame from a fashion VIDEO; the product photos are static studio shots. In a video frame these are NOT defects:
  - motion blur and softness;
  - small text, small logos and fine lettering that are smeared, blob-like or unreadable. A LARGE, prominent logo or wordmark (a big chest print, a large back wordmark) rendered as a clearly WRONG brand or word still rejects: it stays readable in motion;
  - a product seen from another angle or mirrored (a bag held in the other hand, its strap on the other side);
  - a feature hidden or newly visible only because a limb moved (an arm bent, a hand on the hip): pose-occluded, not missing or extra.
  - a sleeve or trouser leg riding up, bunching or swinging with the motion: not a SLEEVE AND LEG LENGTH defect.
VIEW: first decide which side of the person the frame shows: view "front", "back", or "other" (a side view, a close shot, anything else). A product may have a front photo and a back photo: compare it with the photo of the side the frame shows. A side that no photo of a product shows is unknown, not a defect.
VISIBLE: for each product, say whether the frame shows it, even partly (a sleeve, a strap, the tip of a shoe). A product the frame does not show at all has visible false and no defects: it is never a defect here.
Judge only the products listed above and only what this frame shows.
