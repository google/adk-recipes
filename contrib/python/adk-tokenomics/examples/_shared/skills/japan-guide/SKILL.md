# Japan Travel Quick Guide

- **Airport & City Transit**: Load a digital **Suica** or **Pasmo** IC card in Apple/Google Wallet for instant tap-and-go across JR and Metro lines. Skip the nationwide JR Pass unless taking 3+ long-distance Shinkansen trips within 7 days.
- **Best Tokyo Neighborhoods by Style**:
  - **Shinjuku / Shibuya**: Best for first-time visitors, nightlife, and direct airport rail links.
  - **Ueno / Asakusa**: Best value boutique hotels (`$130-$160/night`), traditional temples, and street food.
- **Daily Local Budget**: Budget `$45-$65/day` for food and metro transit (ramen/sushi counters `$10-$18`, teamLab / museum tickets `$25`).
