# Europe City-Break Quick Guide

- **Paris Tips**: Stay in **Le Marais (4th)** or **Saint-Germain (6th)** for walkable cafes and museums, or **Gare de Lyon (12th)** for sub-`$170/night` modern hotels. Book Louvre and Musee d'Orsay timed slots ahead.
- **London & Eurostar**: Tap any contactless card on London Tube/Elizabeth Line (daily cap automatically applied). High-speed **Eurostar** connects London St Pancras and Paris Gare du Nord in `2h 16m`.
- **Daily Local Budget**: Budget `$60-$85/day` for bistro meals, metro passes, and 1-2 major museum entries.
